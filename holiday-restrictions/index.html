Holiday restrictions on oversize travel

States commonly stop oversize and overweight movement around major holidays, even after the permit is paid. The truck waits until the blackout ends.

A holiday restriction is an operating-time rule. It is not a refund and not a fee. The dates and the hours belong to each state. A load that crosses three states in one weekend can be legal in the first, stopped in the second, and legal again in the third.

What still applies when you are allowed to roll

A blackout does not clear a bridge. When the window opens, the load still has to pass structure and routing rules. It may still need escorts or pilot cars. And the ordinary night travel limit is a different clock: a holiday ban and a daylight-only rule can both apply to the same permit.

The estimator prices the permit. It does not know whether Friday is a blackout. Carrier Vehicle Lookup identifies the carrier and the vehicle. It does not publish the holiday calendar.
