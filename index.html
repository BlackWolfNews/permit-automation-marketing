Public pages for oversize moves

These pages answer one question each. They do not issue a permit, clear a route, or price a load.

  • Permit structures and routing rules. What it means to scan a route against bridges and the roads a load is allowed to use.
  • Night travel. Why a daytime permit may not let the truck roll after dark.
  • Escorts and pilot cars. When a load needs a front car, a rear car, or both.
  • Holiday restrictions. Why a paid permit can still sit still on a holiday blackout.

Where the work actually happens

  • Carrier Vehicle Lookup identifies the carrier and the vehicle.
  • Estimator prices the permit. It does not clear a bridge or book an escort.
  • Permit Automation Suite is where you store a truck and trailer and use every tool for one price.

Not legal advice. State rules differ, and they change. A page on this site is an explanation, not a permit.
