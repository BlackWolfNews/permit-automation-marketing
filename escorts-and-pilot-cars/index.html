Escorts and pilot cars

A pilot car runs with the load when the width, length, height, or the route makes the truck too large to move in ordinary traffic alone. States ask for a front escort, a rear escort, or both.

The escort warns traffic, watches clearances, and stays in contact with the driver. It does not replace the permit. The permit fee is not the escort's bill. A load can need one escort in one state and two in the next, on the same trip, because the thresholds are not national.

What this is not

This page does not book pilot cars and does not print a width table. "Over twelve feet needs a front car" is the kind of shortcut that fails at a state line. The requirement follows the dimensions and the route and its structures, and it can change again for night travel.

An escort also does not override a holiday restriction. If the state has stopped oversize movement, the pilot car waits with the load.

The estimator prices the permit. It does not hire the car. Carrier Vehicle Lookup identifies the carrier and the vehicle on the load. It does not assign escorts.

Not a booking tool. Not legal advice. Escort rules differ by state and by route.
